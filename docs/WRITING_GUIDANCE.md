# Where the writing guidance comes from

The skill combines simplified practitioner guidance with workflow design choices
for conversational co-writing. It does not implement any teacher's complete
course, and these references do not establish that the prototype improves jokes.

| Public source | Relevant guidance | How the skill uses a simplified version |
|---|---|---|
| [Greg Dean, Step by Step to Stand-Up Comedy workbook](https://stand-upcomedy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/New-SBS-Bonus-Workbook-2024-PDF-final-2.pdf), chapter 2 | Examine an assumption in a setup, explore another interpretation, and write a punchline that reveals it. | Consider the expectation a line creates and a turn that changes its meaning. |
| [Rik Roberts, Idea to Premise](https://www.schooloflaughs.com/036-live-comedy-writing-session-idea-premise-podcast/) | Explore a topic, narrow the possibilities, then formulate a premise. | Offer distinct angles before selecting one; selection remains provisional. |
| [Jerry Corley, Trying to Write Jokes, but Feeling Stuck?](https://www.standupcomedyclinic.com/trying-to-write-jokes-but-feeling-stuck/) | Find an opinion about a situation and use detail and analogy to develop it. | Start with a concrete observation, perspective, and possible comparison. |
| [Mike Birbiglia, Scriptnotes episode 688](https://johnaugust.com/2025/scriptnotes-episode-688-writing-jokes-with-mike-birbiglia-transcript), discussion of transitions | Work on connections between stories, consider a first-time listener, and compress explanations. | Write actual transitions and check whether the audience has enough context. |

These are optional approaches. A joke need not use every technique or fit one
universal formula. The skill's single-agent interaction, fact-preservation rules,
revision boundaries, and snapshot format are implementation choices.

The repository links to public sources. It does not distribute the referenced
books, worksheets, recordings, or a corpus of other performers' jokes. The worked
example in this repository was generated for illustration. No similarity search
or audience trial establishes its originality or effectiveness.
